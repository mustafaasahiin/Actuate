# Actuate

## Platform
android

## Purpose
Turn a spoken or typed thought into calendar events, list items, tasks and reminders. A capture can produce multiple independent outcomes. Preserve existing user data and local functionality when optional integrations are disconnected.

## Constraints
Native Kotlin / Jetpack Compose with existing domain use cases and repositories. Four destinations: Today, Lists, Calendar, Activity. Settings opens from the header. English app interface. Request permissions in context. Pricing must come from the store. Never invent activity or success.

## Brand commitments
Calm, precise, fast and voice-first. Editorial typography, restrained surfaces, recognizable blue recording control. Accessible light and dark appearances; no simulated waveforms or fabricated progress.

## Evidence
The user's redesign brief is the authority. The working tree contains ongoing product and backend changes which must be preserved. Target audience specifics beyond people capturing everyday actions are undecided.
